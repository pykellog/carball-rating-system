📊 1. Match Results & General Rates
1🏆 Win Rate
Overall Win Percentage
Won ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Indicates the percentage of all matches the player has won. Since teams are randomly formed in CarBall, a rate around 50% is perfectly normal. Values above 50% prove that the player can change the fate of the match and secure a win even when placed in a poorly-performing team.
4,408 ÷ 10,27342.91%
2💀 Loss Rate
Overall Loss Percentage
Lost ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Indicates the percentage of matches lost. A high rate doesn't necessarily mean the player is "bad"; ping differences or inexperienced teammates directly lead to unavoidable losses. It provides a general picture of how often the player falls victim to bad team draws.
5,865 ÷ 10,27357.09%
3⚖️ Win / Loss Ratio
Win to Loss Ratio
Won ÷ Lost
Purpose: Shows how many wins are achieved for every 1 loss. If this number is 1.00, the player wins exactly as much as they lose. If it is below 1, losses outweigh wins in the player's history. It acts as a quick comparison metric between players.
4,408 ÷ 5,8650.752
4📉 Loss / Win Ratio
Loss to Win Ratio
Lost ÷ Won
Purpose: Calculates how many losses the player endures on average to secure 1 win. It is essentially a psychological "grind" metric; the higher this number, the more hardships and uncooperative teams the player has had to play through to achieve victory.
5,865 ÷ 4,4081.330
⏱️ 2. Per Match Averages
5⚽ Goals per Match (GPM)
Average Goals per Match
Goals ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Determines the average number of goals the player scores in a standard 9-minute CarBall match. If you are drafting a "Striker" for your team, you should look directly at this number. Players with a GPM of 1.0 and above are considered elite scorers.
6,537 ÷ 10,2730.636 Goals
6🤝 Assists per Match (APM)
Average Assists per Match
Assists ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Shows how many scoring opportunities the player creates for teammates per match. It's used to identify team players who prefer passing over selfishly shooting at the goal. A high APM indicates exceptional "Playmaker / Midfielder" vision.
3,403 ÷ 10,2730.331 Assists
7🧤 Saves per Match (SPM)
Average Saves per Match (Goalkeeper Score)
Saves ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Shows how many opponent shots are denied per match. In CarBall dynamics, if this number is well above 1.5 (here it is almost 2), it statistically confirms that the player generally takes on the "Goalkeeper" role in their teams. It represents defensive reliability.
19,701 ÷ 10,2731.918 Saves
8🎬 Total Actions
Total Footprint on the Pitch (Workload)
Goals + Assists + Saves
Purpose: The "gross" total of how many positive, scoreboard-changing moves a player has made throughout their entire CarBall career. Instead of merely looking at the match count, this is a volumetric metric showing the "net effort" and experience left on the field.
6,537 + 3,403 + 19,70129,641 Actions
9⚙️ Actions per Match
In-Game Activity (Laziness Test)
Total Actions ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Calculates how much a player stays "active" during a match. A high number (2.5 and above) shows that the player runs everywhere, consistently touches the ball, and doesn't disconnect from the game flow. A low number suggests the player often goes AFK or struggles to get ball touches.
29,641 ÷ 10,2732.885 Actions
⚔️ 3. Offensive Dynamics & Roles
10🎯 Total Contributions
Total Offensive Contribution
Goals + Assists
Purpose: Provides the gross total of offensive production toward the opponent's goal, completely ignoring defense (saves). When calculating a team's total attacking power, you can sum this statistic for your players to immediately gauge the team's offensive potential.
6,537 + 3,4039,940 Contribs
11🗡️ Contributions per Match
Guaranteed Scoring Contribution per Match
Total Contrib ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: Answers the ultimate question: "If I take this player on my team, what do they promise me offensively per match?" Regardless of whether it's a goal or an assist, it is the statistical guarantee of them getting their name on the offensive scoreboard at least once per match.
9,940 ÷ 10,2730.968 Actions
12⚽ Striker Ratio
Forward Tendency (Selfishness / Finishing)
Goals ÷ (Goals + Assists)
Purpose: Shows in how many out of 100 offensive positions the player prefers to shoot rather than pass. If it's over 60%, this player is a pure "Target Striker"; you give them the ball and expect them to run into the penalty box. It clearly reveals their ingame mindset.
6,537 ÷ 9,94065.76%
13🧠 Playmaker Ratio
Playmaker (Passer) Tendency
Assists ÷ (Goals + Assists)
Purpose: The exact opposite of the Striker Ratio. It shows what percentage of the chances they create are passed to teammates. Approaching 50% means this player has excellent "Midfielder" skills and immense game vision, ideal for feeding the strikers in the team.
3,403 ÷ 9,94034.24%
14⚖️ Goals-to-Assists Ratio
Goals Scored per Assist
Goals ÷ Assists
Purpose: Shows how many goals are scored against the opponent for every 1 assist made. It reveals effectiveness in final shots on a clear multiplier level. For example, a result of roughly 2 means they score 2 goals before deciding to make 1 assist.
6,537 ÷ 3,4031.921 Goals
15⚖️ Assists-to-Goals Ratio
Assists Made per Goal
Assists ÷ Goals
Purpose: An indicator of how many assists the player provides to their team for every 1 goal they score themselves. It is very effective for measuring the game vision of defensive players who usually make long passes (clears across the stadium) that turn into immediate assists.
3,403 ÷ 6,5370.521 Assists
🛡️ 4. Offense vs. Defense Role Crossing
16🛡️ Defensive Action Ratio
How Much of Their Life is Spent in Goal?
Saves ÷ Total Actions
Purpose: Calculates what percentage of the player's total ball touches throughout their career were "Goal Saving" actions. If this ratio exceeds 50%, the player's soul is dedicated to defense. When analyzing the opposing team, it allows you to definitively say, "This guy never leaves the goal."
19,701 ÷ 29,64166.47% Defense
17⚔️ Offensive Action Ratio
How Much of Their Life is Spent Upfront?
(Goals+Assists) ÷ Total Actions
Purpose: The opposite of the defensive ratio. It calculates what percentage of the total work done is aimed at finding a goal in the opponent's net. It exposes players who hate staying in goal, always roam upfront, or constantly switch to forward in team rotations.
9,940 ÷ 29,64133.53% Offense
18🧱 Libero Index
Defensive Load Compared to Offense (Static Defender)
Saves ÷ Total Contributions
Purpose: The division of saves by offensive actions. If the result is greater than 1.0, the player defends more than they attack. For instance, in this data, the result is almost 2; meaning for every 1 offensive action, the player saves 2 shots. It represents a "Static Goalkeeper" profile.
19,701 ÷ 9,9401.982 Ratio
19🚀 Forward Index
Offensive Load Compared to Defense
Total Contributions ÷ Saves
Purpose: The exact opposite of the Libero Index. It measures how often a player rushes forward instead of staying in goal. Numbers over 1 are statistical proof that the player has a heavy tendency to leave their goal empty and join the attack.
9,940 ÷ 19,7010.505 Ratio
20🥅 Saves-to-Goals Ratio
Saves Made per Goal Scored
Saves ÷ Goals
Purpose: How many shots must the player save from their own goal until they finally score 1 goal in the opponent's net? In this data, for every 1 goal celebration, 3 dangers were averted in goal. Especially useful for weighing a goalkeeper's offensive capabilities when drafting tactics.
19,701 ÷ 6,5373.013 Saves
21🧤 Saves-to-Assists Ratio
Saves Made per Assist
Saves ÷ Assists
Purpose: The absolute best way to measure how much of a "playmaker" a goalkeeper is. Do they just blindly clear the ball out of bounds, or do they make smart, tactical passes (assists) to teammates, turning defenses into instant counter-attacks? Determines goalkeeper vision.
19,701 ÷ 3,4035.789 Saves
✅ 5. Win Conditioned Effort Metrics
22⚽ Goals per Win
Goals Needed to Secure a Victory
Goals ÷ Won
Purpose: A theoretical calculation; it states the minimum average number of goals this specific player statistically needs to score for their team to win the match. If this number is low, it means teammates can effectively share the burden by scoring goals as well.
6,537 ÷ 4,4081.483 Goals
23🤝 Assists per Win
Assist Effort Required to Win
Assists ÷ Won
Purpose: If the team secures a victory, it shows how many "passes/assists" this player contributed on average to that specific win. Used to understand the pass frequency and impact of the unsung heroes who quietly win matches.
3,403 ÷ 4,4080.772 Assists
24🧤 Saves per Win
Goalkeeper Burden Bringing Victory
Saves ÷ Won
Purpose: Shows the average number of dangers averted in goal during a won match. If it's a high number (like 4.4), it proves that this player wins matches not "comfortably by a huge margin," but by growing into a giant in goal and resisting until the last second.
19,701 ÷ 4,4084.469 Saves
25🔥 Total Actions per Win
The "Sweat Coefficient" of a Victory
Total Actions ÷ Won
Purpose: One of the most critical "Effort" metrics. How many different actions (Goal/Assist/Save) does this player HAVE TO make on the field just to get a single Win? The higher this number, the worse the team draws have been, forcing the player to put in superhuman solo effort to win.
29,641 ÷ 4,4086.724 Actions

6,537 ÷ 5,8651.115 Goals
27🤝 Assists per Loss
Passing Effort During Defeat
Assists ÷ Lost
Purpose: Measures the willpower to not play selfishly (rage mode) in matches lost by a large margin, maintaining team play and continuing to assist teammates regardless of the score. It is a prime indicator of psychological composure.

28🛡️ Saves per Loss
Tragic Defensive Resistance (Under Siege)

Purpose: A Very Important Goalkeeper Metric! Shows how long they held out alone in that goal when the team was practically non-existent and the opposing team constantly fired shots. It is the mathematical proof of the sentence: "I lost a lot because my team was bad, but I also made incredible saves."
19,701 ÷ 5,8653.359 Saves
29🦍 Actions per Loss
The "Never Give Up" Score in Defeats
Total Actions ÷ Lost
Purpose: The pure resilience score of players who don't quit the game (refuse to go AFK) even if the match is 4-0, jumping in goal and seeking goals upfront until the last second. The higher this number, the higher the player's sportsmanship and warrior spirit.
29,641 ÷ 5,8655.054 Actions
👑 7. MVP & "Carry" Metrics
30🏅 MVP / Total Rate
Chance of Being MVP in Any Given Match
MVP ÷ Total Matches
Purpose: If we throw all the matches you played into a pool and pick a random one, what is the probability of you being the MVP? Since only the winning team produces an MVP in FFS and your loss rate is factored in, it is completely normal for this rate to be low (between 10-15%).
1,484 ÷ 10,27314.45%
31👑 MVP / Win Rate
Being the "Star" in Won Matches (Carry Percentage)
MVP ÷ Won
Purpose: ONE OF THE MOST CRITICAL DATA POINTS. Answers the question: "Okay, you won the match, but did your teammates carry you, or were YOU the one who WON the match?" In a 3-player team, if everyone is equal, the rate is 33.3%. This number being 33.67% proves you are the "Alpha" player of the team in victories.
1,484 ÷ 4,40833.67%
32🤝 Non-MVP Win Rate
Victory Rate Brought by Teammates
(Won - MVP) ÷ Won
Purpose: The percentage of matches you won, but someone else in the team (for example, the striker) got the MVP badge. It is the percentage of "harmonious team play" where you successfully played the support or goalkeeper role and allowed your teammate to shine and take the spotlight.
2,924 ÷ 4,40866.33%
33🎯 MVP to Goal Ratio
Ratio of MVP Badges to Goal Frequency
MVP ÷ Goals
Purpose: Measures how often scored goals carry the player to MVP status. For players who exclusively wait upfront and score goals, this ratio is extremely high. However, for those who play mostly as a goalkeeper like keLLog, this ratio being low perfectly confirms their defensive role.
1,484 ÷ 6,5370.227
🔮 8. Advanced Meta-Metrics (Outputs of Outputs)
34🏋️ Carry Index
Carrying a Weak Team Score ("Elo Hell" Index)
(MVP÷Won) × (Lost÷Won)
Purpose: A high-level scout formula. It identifies talented and unlucky players who lose many matches because they constantly fall into weak/novice teams (High Loss), but whenever they finally win a match, they are definitively the best of the team and get the MVP badge. It reveals the player's "pure carry" potential.
0.3367 × 1.3300.448 Score
35🎭 Role Balance Index
Two-Way Player (Box-to-Box) Index
Offensive Ratio ÷ Defensive Ratio
Purpose: Measures whether the player is one-dimensional (only stays in goal or only waits upfront) or a flexible person playing both sides of the pitch. The closer the result is to 1.00, the more this player is a "Versatile All-rounder" Joker. A low number (0.50) proves the player specializes heavily in a specific role (Goalkeeper).
33.53% ÷ 66.47%0.504 Index

When comparing the statistics of two different players, you can directly and flawlessly see who is a more "complete player" just by looking at this OPQI Master Score.
